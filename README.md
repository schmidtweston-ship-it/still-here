# Still Here

My plan for the hard nights. A warm, big-text safety plan that lives on your phone only.

- Make your plan in seven parts: why I keep going (with a photo), warning signs, things I can do on my own, people and places, people I can tell, professionals and crisis lines, and making my space safer.
- "I'm not okay right now" walks you through it one thing at a time. If one doesn't work, pull the next one. What helped comes up first next time.
- Breathe with me, a 5-minute timer, read-aloud, a Show someone screen, a printable card, and a saved backup copy.
- 988 (call or text) and 911 are on every screen.

Not a doctor or a crisis line. Built by Ada for the Schmidt family. Weston and Eve, Equal always.
